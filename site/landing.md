# OntoVibeShift

An ontology-driven data lifecycle engine. It models two or more live business
platforms as one semantic graph, states the relationships between their
vocabularies as reviewable axioms, and drives synchronisation from those
axioms rather than from hand-maintained field mappings.

It is a cloud, where a team works on the graph and the axioms, and an edge,
which runs parts of the lifecycle on a person's own machine and links to the
cloud. Both are built on the D-LAB-5 templates.
